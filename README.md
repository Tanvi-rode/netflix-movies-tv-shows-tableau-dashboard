# Netflix Movies & TV Shows Dashboard (Tableau)

Interactive Tableau dashboard exploring Netflix's content library by type,
country, rating, genre, and release year.

![Dashboard](images/dashboard.png)


## Objective
Understand what kind of content Netflix offers, where it comes from,
who it is aimed at, and how the catalog has grown over time.

## Dashboard Features
- **Type and Title filters:** pick any movie or TV show to see its release
  year, date added, duration, rating, genre, and description
- **Total Movies & TV Shows by Country:** map showing content volume by country
- **Ratings:** number of titles in each rating category
- **Movies and TV Shows Distribution:** share of movies vs TV shows
- **Top 10 Genres:** most common genre combinations
- **Total Movies & TV Shows by Year:** how the catalog grew from 2008 to 2020

## Key Insights
- Movies make up about 68% (4,265) of the catalog and TV shows about 32% (1,969).
- Documentaries (299), Stand-Up Comedy (273), and International Dramas (248)
  are among the most common genres.
- The most common rating is [TV-MA, 2,027 titles], followed by [TV-14, 1,698],
  so most content targets mature and teen audiences.
- Content added grew sharply after 2015 and peaked in 2019.
- The United States and India are among the top content-producing countries.
